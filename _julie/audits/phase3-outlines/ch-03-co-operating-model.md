## Chapter syllogism

- **Premise 1.** You have an operating-model problem, not a technology problem (carried from Ch 2). What the right operating model looks like has not yet been named.
- **Premise 2.** The Co-Operating Model is that structure. It splits the work two ways: by intelligence (humans carry the world model and transmit it; agents execute on what was transmitted) and by ownership (humans own outcomes; agents own tasks and intermediate outputs).
- **Premise 3.** Once the split is designed, work stops moving linearly. Parallel Workstreams (multiple agent-driven tracks running simultaneously under one human's direction) become the default, and the Hybrid Accountability Chart names one human supervisor for every outcome.
- **Conclusion.** Map at least one role against the two columns, compute the Column B percentage and its loaded cost, and you have the structural answer to Ch 2's problem statement. The equation Co-Intelligence + Rhythm = Compound names what the rest of the book builds.

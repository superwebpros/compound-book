## Chapter syllogism

- **Premise 1.** From Ch 1 you accept that your AI shortfall is an operating-model problem, you know your lowest-scoring dimension, and you've seen Meridian as the worked parallel.
- **Premise 2.** You already run on systems (CRM, ERP, project tool, comms stack, doc store). Those systems are friction for humans and feedstock for agents. That makes you a tech company today, not a tech company someday.
- **Premise 3.** Once the tech-company identity is true, three shifts follow: AI is a worker not a tool, the headcount math can bend, your role is to design the work not do it.
- **Premise 4.** The beliefs are load-bearing. Without them the framework reads as extra work for the same outcomes; with them it's the cheapest path to outcomes you already want.
- **Conclusion.** Accept that you're already a tech company and the other three shifts follow. Take stock of where humans are doing work the systems should be doing, and carry that list into Sprint 1.

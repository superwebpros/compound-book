## Chapter syllogism

- **Premise 1.** The reader has read the framework, the Sequence, and the Rhythm. Language and structure are in place; what's missing is the first move.
- **Premise 2.** The first move is constraint-naming, not tool selection. The work of this chapter is a four-instrument sequence: pull the Scorecard to route, run a Minimum Viable Signal to name the constraint with a number, name three team roles, write a one-page plan.
- **Premise 3.** Two paths run that plan forward — self-guided with the book's instruments, or with Compound. The Clarity Call is the optional accelerant on either path.
- **Conclusion.** The reader closes this chapter with a named first move, a cost attached to it, a person who owns it, and a date it starts by.

### E42 (NEW-SECTION, LAND-WITH-MODIFICATION) — "Train the boundary, not the tool" principle as new paragraph inside §"The *per-role runbook*"

**Substance to preserve (from `_julie/julie-redline.md:1908–1928` E42 source + manifest E42 instruction at line 119):**

- The substantive principle: **train the boundary, not the tool**. Most technology training focuses on how to use the new system. The training that prevents adoption failure focuses on **where the human's work ends and the agent's work begins** — the handoff.
- The handoff is the moment of highest failure risk in any human-AI workflow. The human either completes the output correctly and passes it forward, or does it incorrectly and compromises everything the agent produces downstream.
- The **two-question pre-go-live gate**: every person whose handoff changes must be able to answer both questions before the workflow goes live:
  - **What do I do differently now?**
  - **What does the agent do that I used to do?**

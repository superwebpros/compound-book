**Substance to preserve (from `_julie/julie-redline.md:1726–1745`):**

- The substantive insight: TML is **the design logic for Work Deconstruction**. Every task in the constraint workflow falls into one of three layers — Task / Management / Leadership.
- **Task work** = follows defined rules, produces predictable outputs, can be executed by an agent without human input at each step. **This is the Fully Automatable column.**
- **Management work** = requires judgment about process — whether the output meets the standard, whether an exception applies, whether the escalation threshold has been crossed. **This is the AI-Assisted column** (agent executes, human reviews and approves).
- **Leadership work** = requires judgment about people, strategy, relationships, and context that no model can supply from training data alone. **This is the Human Judgment Required column.**
- **The framework's lens-function:** TML doesn't tell you which column is right for every task — it gives you the lens to ask the right question about each one. Not "can the AI do this?" but **"what type of intelligence does this work require, and which type is AI reliably capable of providing at the quality standard this organization needs?"** This is the load-bearing operator beat — preserve verbatim or near-verbatim.
